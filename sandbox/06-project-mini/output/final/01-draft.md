# 01-draft

## Project Summary
Create Obora's sixth canonical sandbox to demonstrate a minimal project lifecycle. The sandbox will produce structured, concise documentation through draft, review, final, validation, and archive phases, ending with a reusable archive note.

## Scope
- In scope:
  - Define a small project lifecycle with draft, review, final, validation, and archive stages
  - Produce short, structured documents at each stage
  - Deliver a reusable archive note as the final artifact
- Out of scope:
  - Large-scale or production-grade workflows
  - Tooling beyond this sandbox demonstration
