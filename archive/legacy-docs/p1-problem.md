## Top 2 Problems Solved

### Problem 1: Task Fragmentation and Lost Productivity

**Description:**
Users struggle to track tasks across multiple contexts (work, personal, shopping, projects), leading to forgotten commitments and reduced productivity. Tasks often exist in memory, scattered notes, or disconnected apps, making it impossible to maintain a unified view of responsibilities.

**Impact:**
- Missed deadlines and forgotten commitments
- Mental overhead from constantly trying to remember tasks
- Inability to prioritize effectively without seeing the complete picture
- Increased stress and reduced sense of control

**User Pain Points:**
- "I forgot to buy groceries on my way home"
- "What was that deadline my boss mentioned?"
- "I feel overwhelmed but don't know what to tackle first"

---

### Problem 2: Lack of Progress Visibility and Motivation

**Description:**
Without a clear system to track completed tasks, users cannot visualize their progress or derive satisfaction from accomplishments. This leads to reduced motivation, procrastination, and a cycle of feeling unproductive despite working on multiple tasks throughout the day.

**Impact:**
- Decreased motivation to complete tasks
- Inability to recognize productivity patterns
- Lack of accountability for long-term goals
- Persistent feeling of "never getting anything done"

**User Pain Points:**
- "I worked all day but feel like I accomplished nothing"
- "I don't know how productive I was this week"
- "I need to see what I've achieved to stay motivated"
- "I lose track of recurring tasks I've already completed"
